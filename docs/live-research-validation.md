# Live research validation — 2026-10-03

This records live/cached service behavior and a manual review of the returned source extracts. It is not independent scientific validation, a systematic review, or evidence that every biological question receives a complete answer.

## Run and reproducibility

The 26-case matrix covers two curated investigations, ten new biological topics, three entity explanations, three intervention mechanism questions, three ambiguous inputs, three restricted operational inputs, an offline investigation, and an injected PubMed failure. The initial live/cached run completed at **2026-10-03 17:16:37 UTC**. The final reviewed run completed at **2026-10-03 22:58:25 UTC**, using the retained source cache after extract-selection changes. The later timestamp is a software-validation time, not a fresh retrieval time; each source keeps its earlier retrieval date.

- Runner: [`scripts/check_research_live.py`](../scripts/check_research_live.py).
- Local run index: `artifacts/research-validation/matrix.json` (local-only).
- Each result is retained as `artifacts/research-validation/<id>.result.json`. Saved cases also have `.html`, `.md`, and `.json` reports and a SQLite snapshot in `sessions.sqlite3`.
- These local artifacts, source caches, and SQLite databases are ignored by Git. They are available in this working checkout and are not a versioned dataset.
- The matrix runs the service and report store directly. HTTP contracts and browser interactions require their separate automated checks; this run alone does not verify them.

## Observed behavior

All 26 cases completed. Twenty results were saved, reloaded with equality to their snapshot, and reopened as HTML, Markdown, and JSON. Ambiguous and restricted inputs returned no source calls or generated claims and were not saved by this runner. Both curated queries retained their embedded curated result, each with three mechanism hypotheses and six evidence records. Their outer universal wrapper intentionally has zero provisional evidence/groups.

The final mode counts were two `CURATED`, fifteen `GENERAL_EVIDENCE_SUMMARY`, three `EXPLANATORY`, three `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED`, and three `RESTRICTED_WITH_SAFE_ALTERNATIVE`. All successful provisional source runs in this rerun used the cache; the injected PubMed failure remained unavailable. The earlier run included ten `LIVE_RESEARCH` outputs, confirming that live records had been obtained before caching. Mode is not a quality grade: cache-only outputs correctly identify cached evidence, while explanatory outputs retain their explanatory mode with source freshness shown separately.

The initial manual review found a material selection defect: prioritizing review publications promoted literature-search methods and peripheral contexts above more relevant biological passages. Revised selection removed that unconditional preference and required closer overlap with the requested topic. Acne now leads with pathogenesis, creatine no longer leads with a creatine-kinase biomarker, and muscle hypertrophy includes relevant mechanotransduction extracts. The final answers still have substantive completeness and context limitations recorded below. Resolving citations is not sufficient to call every answer adequate.

## Integrity and limits

The final artifact audit checked **360 displayed claim instances after deduplicating IDs within each result**, including direct answers, groups, complications, and modifiers. Every audited extract exactly matched its retained source passage and occurred in a cited abstract/annotation. All evidence references resolved. The **276 temporary graph edges** had valid endpoints and claim references; they were source-support links, not inferred molecular causal edges. The 324 provisional evidence records counted across all cases include records reused between investigations and are not 324 independent studies. All 20 saved cases had each report format present.

Species, population, tissue, sample size, and effect estimates commonly remain unknown because the source metadata did not provide them as structured fields. A source paragraph may mention an organism or context without this run performing validated context extraction. UniProt annotations retain their organism and database-entry limitations. No claim here establishes human clinical applicability.

This is bounded retrieval and deterministic extract selection. It can miss relevant studies, retain neighboring topics, mix study contexts, and fail to express the overall biological answer. Review publications can also reuse primary studies. Limiting passages and contradiction searches do not constitute a comprehensive contradiction review. No provisional investigation becomes a curated pack or gains an executable biological model through this process.
