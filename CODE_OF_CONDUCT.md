# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CausalBio Lab contributors are committed to a respectful, inclusive, evidence-oriented community. We welcome people of different backgrounds, identities, disciplines, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique claims, methods, and code without attacking people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, source licenses, and scientific attribution.
- Make room for domain experts and newcomers to ask questions.
- Follow the project's medical-safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, plagiarism, deliberate scientific fabrication, and knowingly unsafe medical or bioactive-substance guidance are not acceptable. Publishing another person's private information without permission is prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone represents the project in public. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates it, and may issue a warning or temporary or permanent ban proportionate to the conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer using a private channel provided by the hosting project. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. No dedicated conduct email is currently published, so this document does not invent one. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, subject to safety and legal obligations. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest should recuse themselves from handling the report.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and adapted for the scientific and medical-safety context of CausalBio Lab.
